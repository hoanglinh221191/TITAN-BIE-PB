# TITAN FMM/M2L Scope Z4L Adaptive Far-Cell Refinement Report

Decision: z4l_accuracy_pass_costly

Z4L evaluates a deterministic p3/p4 local-tail estimator and selectively
splits accepted far cells. The candidate remains sampled-detail attribution
only; production degree-one FMM and GMRES-p4 are unchanged.

Full theta-0.6 results (256 sampled targets):

- 1A63 off: audit=0.00961167, wall=261.35 s.
- 1A63 tol=0.008: audit=0.00969729, refined_cells=8,
  direct_fallback_panels=234, wall=284.09 s.
- 1A63 tol=0.004: audit=0.00936847, refined_cells=111,
  direct_fallback_panels=2719, wall=725.41 s.
- 1A63 tol=0.002: audit=0.00734897, refined_cells=770,
  direct_fallback_panels=15551, wall=1954.01 s.
- Barnase tol=0.002: audit=0.00333376, refined_cells=19,
  direct_fallback_panels=987, wall=1101.75 s.
- 1SH1 tol=0.002: audit=0.00034668, refined_cells=11,
  direct_fallback_panels=572, wall=306.09 s.

The estimator is correlated with the direct audit: tightening the tolerance
from off to 0.002 reduced 1A63 error by about 23.5 percent and crossed the
8e-3 accuracy gate. The same tolerance remained accurate on Barnase and
1SH1. It is not a production candidate: the 1A63 diagnostic runtime grew by
about 7.48x because risky source leaves were moved to direct evaluation.

The next scope, if pursued, should optimize the refinement representation and
order-8 translation path before any guarded GMRES integration. Grid remains
the production Poisson-Boltzmann default, and gmres-p4 remains fail-closed.
