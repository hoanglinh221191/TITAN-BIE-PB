4. Cách viết kết luận về -b=400 ns

Cách viết an toàn: “The first 400 ns were excluded from analysis to minimize early transient effects. The resulting ensemble revealed multiple metastable regions in TICA space and heterogeneous interface stabilization patterns across the four walkers. While this cutoff is sufficient for qualitative mapping of conformational states and interface descriptors, the incomplete overlap between walkers and the absence of a clear ITS plateau indicate that full equilibrium convergence has not yet been established.”

Ý tiếng Việt tương đương: “Việc loại bỏ 400 ns đầu giúp giảm ảnh hưởng của giai đoạn đầu lấy mẫu và cho phép nhận diện rõ nhiều vùng trạng thái metastable cùng các mô thức ổn định interface khác nhau giữa bốn walker. Tuy nhiên, do các walker vẫn chưa chồng lấp hoàn toàn và implied timescales chưa tạo plateau rõ, tập hợp dữ liệu sau cutoff này phù hợp hơn cho phân tích định tính về landscape và cơ chế hơn là cho mô tả cân bằng hoặc động học tuyệt đối.”

5. Đoạn discussion có thể dùng trực tiếp

The multiwalker AWH ensemble analyzed from 400 ns onward reveals a heterogeneous binding landscape rather than a single dominant bound configuration. TICA and UMAP both resolve several metastable regions, with one major basin accompanied by at least three less populated substates. Importantly, the four walkers do not fully overlap in this reduced space: two walkers remain almost entirely within the dominant basin, one walker mainly samples an adjacent substate, and another preferentially visits two alternative regions. This behavior indicates that the 400 ns cutoff is sufficient to capture meaningful post-transient structural heterogeneity, but not yet sufficient to claim complete ensemble convergence.

Interface descriptors support this interpretation. The mean number of intermolecular hydrogen bonds differs strongly among walkers, consistent with distinct interface stabilization modes rather than a unique interaction pattern. In parallel, the average contact map shows only a limited set of contact hotspots, suggesting that complex formation is maintained by selective residue patches rather than by a uniformly packed interface. RMSD and RMSF profiles further indicate a structurally flexible ensemble, while DSSP analysis shows a strong predominance of coil and bend states and only minimal persistent helix or strand content. Together, these results support a model in which binding and partial unbinding proceed through multiple flexible interface arrangements. Therefore, the present dataset is well suited for mechanistic discussion of state heterogeneity and pathway diversity, whereas quantitative equilibrium populations and MSM-derived kinetics should be interpreted with caution.
